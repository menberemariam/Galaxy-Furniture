Policies & promises

Plain-language answers about privacy, terms, warranty, and accessibility.

Privacy

We collect only the details needed to process your order and improve your shopping experience — your name, contact information, delivery address, and order history. Your data is stored securely and is never sold to third parties.

You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by contacting care@galaxyfurniture.et.

Terms of sale

Prices are shown in Ethiopian Birr (ETB) and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. Orders are confirmed once payment or a deposit is received. Custom and made-to-order pieces may take longer to prepare.

Delivery dates are estimates and may vary with location and stock availability. We will contact you if anything changes.

10-year structural warranty

Every chair frame is covered for ten years against manufacturing defects in the structure and joinery. Upholstery, foam, and normal wear are covered for two years.

The 100-day trial lets you live with your purchase. If it is not right, contact us within 100 days to arrange a return or exchange under our trial terms.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to shop with us comfortably. Our showroom has step-free access, and our team is happy to read product details, arrange home visits, or help with orders by phone.

If you meet a barrier on this website, please tell us at care@galaxyfurniture.et so we can fix it.
