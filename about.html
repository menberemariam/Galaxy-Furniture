Our Story

Furniture built for Ethiopian homes, designed for everyday life.

Who We Are

A showroom, a workshop, and a promise of comfort.

Galaxy Furniture started with a simple idea: every family deserves furniture that looks beautiful, feels comfortable, and lasts for years. From our showroom in Addis Ababa, we bring together skilled local craftspeople and carefully selected materials.

Whether you are furnishing a first apartment or refreshing a family living room, our team helps you choose pieces that fit your space, your budget, and the way you live.
